  • Leftfoto Camera Rail Nodal Slide It's a knock-off of the original, but works just fine for a fraction of the cost.

  • Smallrig foldable L-bracket Great design, although the vertical clamp is a bit thinner than the horizontal, so be aware if you use a lever clamp. It might close tightly when in horizontal mode, but might be a bit loose when vertical. No issues if you use a knob closure system. Still a bit heavy for my taste, so I'm waiting for a better solution, even if not foldable.
  • 3-Legged Thing Ellie PD I've been using Peak Design Capture clips for years. Although I recently replaced mine with a Falcam F38 Clip for comfort reasons, a clip remains my main way of carrying my gear when I'm out and about shooting outside. Using an L-bracket, though, makes it impossible to put the camera on the clips, as they are compatible with the plates, but not with a full dovetail rail. I tried different solutions, but none worked, so I ended up storing the L-bracket in my bag for when I'm using the tripod, and the rest of the time using the capture clip. The new field plate makes it a breeze as a tool-less solution. Then came 3-Legged Thing: in collaboration with PD, they built the plate into the bracket itself. Now it's the best of both worlds. The Smallrig foldable is a great bracket, but it's heavy and is not compatible with the clips. So I finally found the better solution.
  • SmallRig HawkLock Quick Release Half Cage I really thought camera cages were exclusively for videographers. They looked like a good idea, but I thought I'd never get one for my camera. Until I saw this video from Mike Smith explaining how to DIY the perfect L-bracket that won't interfere with your rear screen or connectors, and still make it Peak Design clip compatible. Just a brilliant idea.
  • Falcam F22 and F38 Quick Release L-Bracket Then, after too much research and trial and error, it turns out that Falcam already fixed the problem with a specific L-Bracket that looks like a half cage, and it has some of its features, but you don't need to add extra plates. A bit heavier than the half cage, but if you take into account the couple of plates you need to add to have full functionality, it ends up being a better solution as it provides an extended surface to land your pinky finger.
